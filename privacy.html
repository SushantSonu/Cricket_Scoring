Privacy Policy

Cric Score · Last updated August 14, 2026

Cric Score is a cricket scoring app built for casual and gully (street) cricket matches. This policy explains how the app handles your data — in short: it doesn't collect any.

No account, no server, no collection

Cric Score has no user accounts, no backend server, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. The app runs entirely on your device. Match data — team names, scores, player names, and match history — is stored only in your device's local storage / app storage, and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

What data the app stores, and where

  • Match data (teams, overs, scores, batting/bowling figures, match history): stored locally on your device only.
  • "Continue on another device" links: if you choose to generate one, the match data is encoded directly into the link itself. That link is created and shared entirely at your discretion — we never see or store it.

We do not have access to any of this data. We cannot see it, and cannot recover it if you clear your browser/app data.

Permissions

Cric Score does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or any other device permission beyond what's needed to run as an installed app.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The in-app "Ask about cricket" feature answers common cricket-rule questions using a fixed, built-in list of answers; if it doesn't recognize a question, it offers a link to search that question on Google in your browser — no data is sent anywhere by the app itself in the process.

Children's privacy

Cric Score does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.

Contact
